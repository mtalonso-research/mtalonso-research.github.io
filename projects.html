Book Reader: translate, narrate, and summarize your books

v1.0 · macOS (Apple Silicon)

A local-first desktop app that turns PDFs, EPUBs, and Word documents into translated text, summaries, and audiobook-style narration. Everything runs on your own machine, so your documents never leave it.

Sample text: The Happy Prince and Other Tales by Oscar Wilde, public domain, via Project Gutenberg.

  • Chapter-aware reading. Imports PDF, DOCX, EPUB, TXT, and Markdown and detects chapters and sections, so you can work one chapter at a time.
  • Translation with local LLMs. Runs models through Ollama, with a glossary for consistent terminology and automatic quality checks that catch bad outputs and retry them.
  • Narration. Neural text-to-speech with downloadable voices, plus an optional higher-quality voice engine installed on demand. Narrate in the background and export to MP3.
  • Built as a real product. Background job queue, per-user data storage, an English and Spanish interface, automated tests, and a packaged macOS app.

Meal Planner: plan your week, build your grocery list

v1.0 · macOS (Apple Silicon)

A local-first desktop app that plans a week of meals from your own recipe library, balances them against your goals, and turns the plan into a grocery list. Recipes and plans stay on your machine, and the AI features run on a local model.

Screenshots use the app's built-in sample recipes.

  • Weekly planner. A 5- or 7-day grid of breakfast, lunch, snack, and dinner. Lock the meals you like and auto-fill the rest, with limits on prep time, required tags, and how soon a recipe can repeat.
  • Goals and nutrition. Balanced, high-protein, or low-carb planning against a daily calorie target, with per-recipe macros. Dishes can be set as complete meals, mains, or sides, and paired into main + side meals.
  • Recipes from anywhere. Add recipes by hand, import them from a web link (structured recipe data first, with a local model as fallback), or generate them with a local LLM through Ollama.
  • Grocery list and pantry. Combines ingredients across the week by aisle, exports as a checklist, text file, or QR code for your phone, and suggests recipes you can make from what is already in the pantry.
  • Built as a real product. Typed end to end, validated recipe data, a local SQLite database, light and dark themes, automated tests, and a packaged macOS app.
