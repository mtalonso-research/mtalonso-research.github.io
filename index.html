About

Hi, I’m Marian. At United Data Technologies I build components of a private enterprise AI platform that lets organizations run large language models and multimodal AI on their own data and infrastructure.

I also hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Florida International University, where I was part of the solid Lab (Security, Optimization, and Learning for InterDependent networks) at the Knight Foundation School of Computing and Information Sciences. My research focused on information theory, signal processing, and machine learning for energy-efficient compression, communication, and inference, including task-based quantization, nonlinear analog processing in wireless systems, sensor fusion, and deep learning for multi-modal CSI compression, published at IEEE venues such as ISIT and ITW and in IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology. I spent two years as a research intern at Sandia National Laboratories, analyzing large-scale power grid simulation data. I am a recipient of the GAANN fellowship, a Grace Hopper Celebration Scholar, and an honorary member of the IEEE Information Theory Society Student and Outreach (S&O) subcommittee.
