Bio

Marian Temprana Alonso is an AI software engineer in the R&D group at United Data Technologies, where she builds components of a private enterprise AI platform that lets organizations run large language models and multimodal AI on their own data and infrastructure. Her work spans LLM fine-tuning, retrieval, agentic systems, safety guardrails, and image and video generation.

She holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Florida International University, where she was part of the Security, Optimization, and Learning for InterDependent networks laboratory (solid lab) in the Knight Foundation School of Computing and Information Sciences, working under the supervision of Dr. M. Hadi Amini and Dr. Farhad Shirani. Her research focused on information theory, signal processing, and machine learning, with an emphasis on energy-efficient compression, communications, and sensing. Her projects covered task-based quantization, wireless communications with nonlinear analog processing, accuracy and consensus-based sensor fusion, and deep learning-driven multi-modal sensor fusion for CSI compression, leading to publications at IEEE venues such as ISIT and ITW and in IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology. Her recognitions include a Graduate Assistance in Areas of National Need (GAANN) Fellowship and being part of the winning team for the ISIT 2023 DeepVerse 6G Machine Learning Challenge. She also spent two years as a research intern at Sandia National Laboratories, applying data analytics and predictive modeling to power grid resilience.
